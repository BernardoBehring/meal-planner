# Build the Shopping list

- **Shopping list** = total consumption − Projected stock, rounded up to real pack sizes, with a small margin for staples that are 🟡 or 🔴.
- Do not buy what is already at home in sufficient quantity.
- A bigger pack only pays off if the quantity will be eaten without waste (consider Stock and expiry dates).
- Product names exactly as they appear on the Family's local shelves.
- Group by supermarket (per the purchase decision of `price-compare-stores`) and by how it is bought (e.g. an online order vs. on foot, when the profile says so), then by category (Protein, Fruit, Vegetables, Dairy, Pantry, Frozen…), each item with ☐ and quantity.
