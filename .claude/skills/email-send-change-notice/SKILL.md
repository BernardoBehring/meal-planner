# Change notice

1. Send to the Lead (type "all") an e-mail with before and after, subject `⚠️ <"Meal planner change" in the conversation language>: <budget | recipients>`.
2. If the Lead's address changed, send the notice to the **old** address, so a change made by someone else cannot be hidden.
3. Record the change, dated, under PENDING until the next Plan, which shows it in the e-mail; remove it after that Plan.
