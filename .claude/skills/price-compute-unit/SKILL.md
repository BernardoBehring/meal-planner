# Real cost

- Always calculate the **price per kg/l/unit**: that is how different packs are compared.
- When it makes sense, also the **cost per portion** (using the Menu's portion sizes).
- A bigger pack only pays off if the price per kg is lower **and** the quantity will be eaten without waste (consider Stock and expiry dates).

Output: Price/kg-l-unit (e.g. 5.99/kg) and cost per portion.
