# Adult nutrition estimate

Each Adult may have individual goals (e.g. losing or gaining weight). When the profile has enough data (sex, height, weight, activity, goal), estimate per day, per Adult: kcal, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fibre, from the portions on the Menu.

- Always label it **PLANNING ESTIMATE**, never a medical prescription.
- Adjust the Adult's portions to the goal (e.g. a moderate deficit for weight loss, protein kept up) without dropping fruit, vegetables or meals.
- Never for Children (see `menu-check-children`).

Output: the "Adults" section of the Week file: portions per meal and the daily estimate per Adult, by name.
