# Child profile

School and hours, where they have lunch on school days (canteen or lunchbox; the canteen only changes Presence), whether they take a snack from home, appetite, sport, allergies and intolerances. Ask what they eat and like, never their weight for diet purposes (`menu-check-children`).

→ the Member's block (Child template in `templates/state.md`).
