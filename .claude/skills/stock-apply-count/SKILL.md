# Apply a Stock count

From the photos (or the list the Lead typed), identify the foods and approximate quantities. **Rewrite** the STOCK section from them (it replaces the calculated Stock), in the `stock-classify` format, and update "Last Stock count".

- Whatever cannot be seen in the photos (e.g. the back of the freezer) stays as it was, marked "not checked in the Stock count".
- Output the main differences from the calculated Stock, for the Week file's Review section. Those differences show where the system was wrong (e.g. fruit consumption higher than planned).
