# Unconfirmed purchases

A Shopping list purchase without a Receipt stays **Unconfirmed**: the system assumes it was made and uses the Estimated cost in place of the Spend, labelled **ESTIMATE**, until the Receipt arrives. The Receipt goes under PENDING.

If the Lead reports a Spend without a Receipt (e.g. in a Review), record it as "Spend reported (no Receipt)" instead of the Estimated cost, keeping the Receipt pending.
