# Record the Plan in BUDGET

In BUDGET in `data/state.md`, under the purchase month: the planned Week's line `Week of YYYY-MM-DD (purchase YYYY-MM-DD): Estimated cost XX.XX | Spend — (list — + top-up —)`, and "Next Week budget" from `budget-compute-week`.
