# Plan the meal prep

Per ROUTINES: on the days and with the time the Family gave (by default, the day after the Shopping day, the first day of the Week).

- Each prepared dish should serve meals on the following days; reuse it (e.g. roast chicken on the prep day → next day's lunchbox → wrap filling later). Mark those meals ♻️ in the Menu.
- Respect what the profile says about storage (fridge and freezer space) and about dishes the Family does not like reheated.
- If the Family does **not** do meal prep, plan no prep: check instead that each day's meals fit that day's cooking time (PROFILE / Kitchen), with quicker dishes on work days.

Output: the Meal prep table (Dish | Quantity | Storage | Eat by | Used in), or "The Family does not do meal prep".
