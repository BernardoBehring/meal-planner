# Default monthly Budget

1. If the Lead gave no monthly limit, use **weekly × 4.33** as a reference, labelled "estimated from weekly".
2. Check the weekly Budget is realistic **for the number of Members, their ages and the local cost of food** (e.g. two teenagers eat more than two small children). If it looks insufficient for adequate food, say so with numbers, without hiding it.
