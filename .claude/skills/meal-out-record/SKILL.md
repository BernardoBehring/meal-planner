# Meals out

- **Meal out** (restaurant, fast food, takeaway, ready food eaten out): record under MEALS OUT (date, place, amount). It **does not count in the Budget** and does not go into Stock.
- **School and routine work canteens** are **not** Meals out: if a canteen receipt shows up, only note it under PENDING as "fixed cost, outside the system".
