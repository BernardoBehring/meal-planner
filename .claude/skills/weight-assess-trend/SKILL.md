# Weight trend

Use averages, not single days: daily weight swings with water and salt.

- **Weekly** (Plan): record in the Week file's Review the 7-day average, its change from the previous 7 days and from the start of the log.
- **Monthly** (month close): the 7-day average at the start and end of the month, and the change.
- If that Adult's goal is weight loss and the 7-day average has not dropped for **3 Weeks in a row**, adjust their portions a little (less carbohydrate, same protein, fruit and vegetables) and say so.
- A loss faster than about **1% of body weight per week** is worth mentioning too.
- Adults only, never Children.
