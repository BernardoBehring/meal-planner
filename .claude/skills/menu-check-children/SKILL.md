# Children's rules

Children (under 18, see `presence-compute`) are part of the family plan but have their own rules, adjusted to each one's age. Their food prioritises growth, development, adequate energy, protein, iron, calcium, vitamin D, fruit, vegetables, grains, healthy fats, hydration, variety and good habits. Teenagers in a growth spurt or doing sport often need more energy than a sedentary Adult.

Reject, and fix, anything that applies to a Child:

- calorie deficit, weight-loss goals or diets, fasting, food restriction, obsessive calorie counting, restrictive low-carb diets or cutting out food groups;
- a calorie target — use portions suited to their age, appetite and routine, and let them recognise hunger and fullness cues;
- a saving that reduces their essential foods (dairy or alternative every day, fruit, vegetables, protein).

Talk about Children with care: ask what they eat and like, never their weight for diet purposes. If the Lead raises a concern about a Child's weight, growth, development or health, record it and recommend a paediatrician or paediatric dietitian.

## Cases that need a health professional

For babies under 2, pregnancy, breastfeeding, older people with specific needs, chronic conditions (e.g. diabetes, kidney disease, severe allergies) or prescribed diets (any Member, Adult or Child): plan the Family's food in general, but say clearly that that Member's needs must be set by a health professional, and follow their guidance when the Lead shares it.
